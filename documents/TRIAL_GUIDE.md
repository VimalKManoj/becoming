# Two-week personal trial (Phase 5C)

The point of the trial: use Becoming for two ordinary weeks and find out whether it reduces the effort of choosing what to do, and helps work get finished. Base any change afterwards on what you observed, not on what seemed likely. The PRD's questions are the guide: did the suggestion fit tonight, did balancing help or interrupt momentum, was the streak encouraging, and which screen did you actually return to?

## Before day one

1. Run `npm run backend` once so your development deployment has the latest functions and schema, then `npm run dev`.
2. In **Settings**, choose your timezone and a weekly target (three is only a suggestion). Leave lane balance on "Equal attention" for the first week so you test the default rules.
3. In **Work**, make sure each lane has two or three Ready tasks with honest minutes and energy. Give larger tasks a smaller step so short evenings still have an option.
4. Optional: start one project with a milestone, so the case-study draft has something to assemble at the end.

## Each evening (about a minute)

- Open **Today**, choose your time and energy, and notice how long choosing took.
- If you pick an alternative, tap the reason that fits ("Too big tonight", "Not in the mood"…). It's recorded with the session and changes nothing else.
- Recap honestly: Finished, Made progress or Blocked, with a real next step. Add evidence when you have it, then or later from Journey.
- If you skip an evening on purpose, that's fine. If you know a whole week will be off, plan a pause in Settings.

Keep a short note anywhere you like (an idea in Ideas works) about anything that felt awkward, slow or surprising.

## What the app records for you

Nothing here is analytics, and nothing leaves your account:
- **Journey → Suggestions:** how many sessions followed Today's top suggestion, and the most common swap reason, for the sessions shown. Journey loads 12 at first; press Show older sessions to include more.
- **Journey → Estimates:** planned against actual minutes for timed sessions, for the sessions shown.
- **Journey → Weekly rhythm:** each week's result and your streak, plus a reflection for this week or last.
- **Journey → Balance:** sessions per lane, for the sessions shown.
- **Journey → The last twelve weeks:** which days had a contribution.

## At the end of the two weeks

Write down, in Journey's reflections or a draft:
1. Typical time to choose a focus, and whether it was usually under a minute.
2. How often you followed the suggestion, and the main reason when you didn't.
3. Whether lane balancing helped or got in the way of finishing a project milestone. That decides whether to try "Favour Projects".
4. Whether your estimates ran over, and by roughly how much.
5. Which screens you returned to without being reminded.
6. Any upkeep that felt like a chore (maintaining tasks, recaps, evidence).

Then choose at most two focused changes. Export a backup from Settings first (Download backup), so you can always compare with this period.

## Turning the trial into portfolio evidence

The trial is itself a case study: the problem, what you built, how it behaved under real use, what you changed and why. For the factual timeline, use a project's **Case study draft** in Work → Projects, then add the reasoning yourself. Becoming never posts anything for you.
